OUR MISSION

Helping people get outside.

Trail & Timber exists to make outdoor adventures more accessible by providing dependable equipment for hikers, campers, and explorers.

We focus on practical products that are easy to use, built for adventure, and ready for whatever the trail brings.

OUR STORY

Born from a love of exploration.

Trail & Timber started with a simple idea: outdoor gear should help people experience nature instead of getting in the way.

From quiet weekend camping trips to long days on the trail, our goal is to help every customer feel prepared for their next adventure.
